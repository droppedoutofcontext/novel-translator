Chapter 2: An Answer from the Sea

In the morning, Mara found a basket of apples and a folded note on the doorstep.

“Your light was visible even beyond the North Spit. Thank you for waiting.”

There was no signature. Instead, the paper bore a drawing of a boat with two uneven sails.

Mara pinned the note above her desk. Then she washed the lantern glass, though it was almost clean. Evening was still a long way off.

“We will light it early today as well,” she decided.
