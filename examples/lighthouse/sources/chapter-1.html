Chapter 1: The Light in the Fog

Mara lit the lantern earlier than usual. Fog had already hidden the North Spit, and the fishing boat had not returned.

“Then we will keep the light on until morning,” she told the empty room.

The watch bell rang once. Mara set her warm gloves beside the lamp and opened the logbook. Someone had drawn a small bird on the last page.

* * *

By midnight, the wind had fallen. A short horn sounded through the grey curtain. Mara went to the window and raised a second lantern.

Far out at sea, a tiny light answered.
